FAQ

Good to know before you book.

Is this really for complete beginners?+
Yes — Survival Swim and Basic Swim are both designed for zero prior experience. Every drill is built step by step so nothing feels rushed.
I'm scared of deep water — will I still learn?+
Absolutely. That fear is exactly what Survival Swim is built around. Progression is gradual and coach-led the entire time — you're never pushed past what you're ready for.
Is there an age limit?+
Programs are open to ages 15 and up, with no upper limit — students in their 50s and 60s train with us regularly.
Can I join if I'm not in Dubai?+
In-person lessons are Dubai-only for now, but online coaching — technique review, freediving theory, and remote programming — is available wherever you are.
Can I just walk in?+
No — Swim Hub runs on pre-booking only, so your coach and pool slot are reserved specifically for you.
